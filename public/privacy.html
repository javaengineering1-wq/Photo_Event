What this app is

Photo Contest lets a host run a photo contest for an event: guests upload photos during the event and vote on their favorites afterward. This page explains what information is collected, from hosts and from guests, and how to have it deleted.

Information hosts provide

When you create a host account, we store your email address and a securely hashed version of your password (we never store your password itself in readable form). When you create an event, we store the event name and the timing you set.

Information guests provide

When a guest joins an event, they choose a username and a 4-digit PIN for that event. The PIN is stored as a securely hashed value, never in readable form. Guests are identified only by the name they choose — this app does not ask guests for email addresses, phone numbers, or any other real-world identity information.

Photos

Photos guests upload are stored so they can be shown in that event's album and downloaded by the host or by guests once results are in. Photos are visible to anyone with that specific event's guest link while the event is active, and to the event's host at any time.

What we don't do

This app does not show ads, does not sell or share your data with third parties for advertising, and does not use analytics or tracking scripts. The only outside party involved is our hosting provider, which stores the data described above on our behalf in order to run the service.

How long data is kept

Event data (guest lists, photos, likes) is kept until the host resets or deletes that event, or deletes their account entirely. Host account data is kept until the host deletes their account.

Deleting your data

Hosts can delete their account and all associated data — every event, every photo, every guest list — at any time from within the app, or from this page without needing the app installed. Deleting a host account is immediate and cannot be undone.

Guests don't have a persistent account to delete, but a guest's name and PIN for a specific event are removed if the host removes that name from the event's guest list, or if the host deletes or resets the event.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children and is not knowingly used to collect information from children.
